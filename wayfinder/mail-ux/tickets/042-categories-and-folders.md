## Question

[Folders, labels, or both](004-folders-and-labels.md) settled that Mail has
folders, and what Archive, Move, Trash and the Folders tick menu do. It left
one question: where the Inbox's categories (Updates, Promotions, Social,
Forums) fit. Are they parts of the Inbox, or folders of their own? Decide
whether they appear in the Folders tick menu, what moving a mail between
them means (re-sorting it, with the per-message category move and its sender
rule, or filing it), and how Archive and Move from a category tab read under
004's rules.

Proposed in 004: categories stay parts of the Inbox, never folders; they are
left out of the tick menu, and moving a mail between them re-sorts it rather
than filing it. Archive from a category tab already follows 004's one rule.

Held on 2026-10-15 at the user's request until Sagar's work on categories is
done; take it up then.
