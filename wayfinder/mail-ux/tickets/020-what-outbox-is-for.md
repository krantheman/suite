## Question

Outbox mixes delivery jargon ("Final" against "Sent", "Submissions", SMTP
codes), says nothing about how long it keeps items, and its "Remove" is
ambiguous. Decide what Outbox is for a user (sending in progress, scheduled
mail, failures, delivery receipts), what it shows by default, what is
hidden behind details, and its words. Inputs: C23 in [merged problems](../references/merged-problems.md) (A64 to A68).
