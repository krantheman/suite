## Question

A failed save or a dropped connection can lose what you wrote, and nothing
says whether the draft is saved or offers recovery. Decide what a user is
told about a draft's save state, what happens when saving fails or the
connection drops, and whether a local copy is kept for recovery. Inputs:
C22 in [merged problems](../references/merged-problems.md) (I13).
