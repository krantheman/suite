## Question

A forward starts a new thread by default, so the conversation splits: the
forward and its replies sit apart from the mail they are about. An account
setting, Keep Forwarded Email In Thread, keeps them together (it adds
In-Reply-To pointing at the original), but it is off by default and buried
in Account settings. Decide what a forward should do: stay in the original
thread by default, and whether the setting stays, flips, or goes. Settle
what the recipient's client sees (a forward that threads with mail they
never had) and how a forward to a new audience reads in the sender's thread.

Source: the user's pain list (2026-10-09).
