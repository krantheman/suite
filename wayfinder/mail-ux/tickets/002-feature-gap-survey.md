## Question

What do Gmail, Superhuman, HEY, Fastmail, Apple Mail and Spark offer that
Suite Mail lacks, and which of those gaps matter for work mail? For each gap:
what it is, which products have it, how they present it, whether Stalwart or
JMAP can carry it (or what it would need), and a rough value for the users in
this map's Notes. Findings go in `references/feature-gap-survey.md`.

## Answer

Resolved 2026-10-09 by a research agent. Findings, with sources:
[references/feature-gap-survey.md](../references/feature-gap-survey.md),
29 gaps (G01 to G29).

Top gaps by value and feasibility: categories UI (backend exists),
unsubscribe with a subscriptions manager, a visual rule builder over Sieve,
templates, mobile swipe actions, snooze and follow-up reminders (a Frappe
scheduler; Stalwart has no snooze), mute thread, shared inboxes and
delegation, per-sender and VIP notifications, print, saved searches.

AI and translation need an outside service, so they are a policy decision
first. Pixel-based open tracking is recommended against. Competitors do the
screener, palette, search, tracker protection and sender stacks better.
