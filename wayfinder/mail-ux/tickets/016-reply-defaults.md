## Question

The only quick-reply icon on a message is Reply All, even on group aliases,
and on desktop Reply, Reply All and Forward only sit after the last message,
so replying means scrolling and replying to everyone is the reflex. Decide
what replying offers by default (per message and per thread), where the
reply controls live, and when Reply All is the right default. Show several
variants in the running app. Inputs: C07 in [merged problems](../references/merged-problems.md) (A21, A22).
