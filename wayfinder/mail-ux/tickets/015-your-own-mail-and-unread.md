## Question

Mail you wrote can become unread (Mark as Unread on a conversation, Mark
Unread from Here, some imports), and Sent shows an unread count. Decide
what "unread" means for your own mail: whether actions may un-read it at
all, whether Sent (or any folder that holds only your mail) shows a count,
and what to do with mail already marked unread. Facts:
[Sent mail shows as unread](007-sent-mail-shows-unread.md).
