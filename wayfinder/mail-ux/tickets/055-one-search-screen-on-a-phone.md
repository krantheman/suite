## Question

A phone has two search screens with different rows. The results page has a read-only field with no back, clear, count or filters.

Proposed: One screen: back, the query, clear, a sideways filter row, a count, and the same rows. The Search key only hides the keyboard.

Source: a study of search on 2026-11-23, with a prototype at https://sketch.netchamp.dev/u/krantheman/mail-search-gaps (the Tweak panel flips each fix back to today's). Priority is the user's to set.
