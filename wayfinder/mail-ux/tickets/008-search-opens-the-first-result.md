## Question

Running a search opens the first result straight away, so the results list
is half hidden behind a thread you didn't pick, and that thread gets marked
read. Decide what a search shows first: the results alone until one is
chosen, on desktop and on a phone, and how the keyboard moves through them.
Check it against how opening a mailbox behaves, so the two agree.

Source: the user's pain list (2026-10-09).

## Inputs

From [merged problems](../references/merged-problems.md) (ticket 003):
- C28 Search opens a result you didn't pick (A51).

From the search study of 2026-11-23: Enter in the palette could show all the results, with
"All N conversations" as its first row, rather than open the first one. See the prototype in
[Search shows why a row matched](051-search-shows-why-a-row-matched.md).
